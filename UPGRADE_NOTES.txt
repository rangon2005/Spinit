Spinit 2.0 latest UI/game update

- Truth or Dare is fixed to exactly 10 rounds; the round slider was removed.
- The Mode selector remains available.
- All mini-games are now behind one Games slide/dropdown button.
- Quick Ludo uses the user-provided real Ludo board image at chat-backgrounds/ludo-board.jpg.
- Ludo has 2 pawns per player, standard 52-space-style movement, 6-to-enter, safe squares, captures, exact home finish, 15s turns, dice animation and pawn movement animation.
- Ludo supports 2-4 players in normal rooms; Quick Match remains a 2-player match.
- Ludo player names are shown beside their assigned red/green/yellow/blue side.
- Chat is kept at the bottom only when the user is already near the bottom, avoiding forced scrolling while reading history.
- Selecting a sticker/GIF/photo closes the attachment/picker drawer automatically.
- Chat backgrounds use the four supplied background files and an absolute same-origin asset URL for reliable Vercel loading.
- Existing README, config, package and env-example files remain unchanged.
